What Changed in Version 2?

Version 2 adds three major capabilities that were missing in Version 1: a complete onboarding and payment system, a referral and commission program, and a full admin management suite. These updates turn the platform from a prototype into a production-ready system that can onboard strategists, process payments, track referrals, and manage the entire community.

1. Onboarding & Payment System

What it does: Strategists can now register, complete a 7-step onboarding process, select a fellowship plan, and pay online — all within the platform.

Before Version 2

  • Users could register with email and password only
  • No fellowship plan selection
  • No online payment processing
  • No legal agreement signing
  • Admin had to manually set up each new user

After Version 2

  • 7-step guided onboarding — Collects personal details, fellowship selection, legal agreements, and payment
  • Two fellowship plans — Standard ($1,200) and Plus ($1,500)
  • PayPal payment processing — Secure payments via PayPal
  • Legal agreements — 7 required terms with checkbox acceptance
  • Automatic account activation — Users become active immediately after payment

How the Onboarding Works

Step 1: Create account and enter personal details (name, phone, location, LinkedIn, areas of interest)

Step 2: Review and confirm your information

Step 3: Choose your fellowship plan (Standard or Plus)

Step 4: Review the fellowship agreement

Step 5: Accept 7 required terms and conditions

Step 6: Pay securely via PayPal

Step 7: Receive confirmation and next steps

2. Referral & Commission Program

What it does: Strategists earn $50 for every new member they refer who completes payment. The system tracks referrals, manages a wallet, and handles payouts.

How It Works

1. Get Your Code — Each strategist receives a unique referral code (e.g., TBP-JOHN-2026)
2. Share It — Send your code to colleagues and contacts
3. Someone Registers — When they sign up using your code, they are linked to you
4. They Pay — Once they complete their fellowship payment, you earn a $50 credit
5. 30-Day Hold — Credits are held for 30 days to ensure payment validity
6. Auto-Approval — After 30 days, the credit is automatically added to your wallet
7. Get Paid — Request a payout when your balance reaches $100 or more

3. Admin Management Suite

What it does: A complete set of tools for administrators to manage users, projects, referrals, payments, and the overall platform.

User Management

  • Add users — Create accounts for new strategists
  • Change roles — Promote or change user roles (Admin, Strategist, Corporate, etc.)
  • Change stages — Move users through the 6-stage progression pathway
  • Reset passwords — Help users who are locked out
  • Assign work areas — Match strategists to their areas of expertise
  • Deactivate users — Remove access when needed

Platform Analytics

  • User growth charts — See how the community is growing
  • Project statistics — Track active projects and progress
  • Budget analysis — Monitor project budgets and spending
  • System health — View uptime, memory usage, and database counts

Referral & Payment Management

  • Referral dashboard — View all referrals and their statuses
  • Payout processing — Approve, process, or reject payout requests
  • Audit trail — Complete history of all referral and payment actions
  • Code generation — Generate referral codes for users in bulk
