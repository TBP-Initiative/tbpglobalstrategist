1. Platform Overview

The TBP Global Strategist platform is your central workspace for managing your journey through the TBP Strategist Pathway. Key areas include:

  • Marketing Site — Public pages including Home, About, Contact, Insights, Initiatives, Innovation, Partners, Research, and Summit
  • Individual Dashboard — Your personalised home page with stats, pathway information, projects, activity feed, and submission tools
  • Profile — Your professional profile visible to other strategists and leadership
  • Projects — Browse and contribute to TBP projects across 6 core categories
  • Messages — Direct messaging and conversations with other strategists
  • Strategists — Explore profiles of other strategists in the network
  • Admin Panel — (Admin users only) Manage users, stages, work areas, and system settings

2. Authentication & Login

2.1 Logging In

  1. Navigate to https://www.tbpglobalstrategist.com/login
  2. Enter your email address and password
  3. Click Sign In

2.2 Google Sign-In

You can also sign in using your Google account by clicking the Continue with Google button on the login page.

2.3 Creating an Account

  1. Navigate to https://www.tbpglobalstrategist.com/register
  2. Enter your full name, email address, and password
  3. Click Create Account
  4. You will be redirected to your dashboard
Note: New accounts start at Stage 1 (Global Strategist Candidate). An administrator must progress you through stages.

3. Onboarding & Fellowship

After creating your account, you will be guided through a 7-step onboarding process to complete your profile, select a fellowship pathway, and process your payment.

3.1 Onboarding Steps

Step 1: Participant Details

Provide your personal information including:

  • Full name and preferred name
  • Phone number
  • City and country
  • LinkedIn URL
  • Current status (student, professional, executive, etc.)
  • Areas of interest (multiple selection available)
  • Referral code (if you were referred by another strategist)

Step 2: Overview

Review your submitted information and confirm accuracy before proceeding.

Step 3: Fellowship Pathway

Choose between two fellowship pathways:

PathwayAmountBenefits
Standard$1,200Core fellowship benefits including platform access, work area assignment, and progression pathway
Plus$1,500Enhanced fellowship with additional benefits, priority project assignment, and extended support

Step 4: Agreement

Review the TBP fellowship agreement terms and conditions.

Step 5: Terms Signing

Accept 7 required terms by checking each checkbox:

  • Code of Conduct agreement
  • Intellectual Property acknowledgment
  • Privacy Policy acceptance
  • Refund Policy acknowledgment
  • Accuracy declaration
  • Additional terms and conditions
  • Final agreement confirmation

Step 6: Payment

Complete your fellowship payment via PayPal:

ProviderMethodsDescription
PayPalPayPal Balance, Cards, BankPayPal checkout with multiple payment options
Note: All payment information is encrypted and processed securely through PayPal. We never store your payment details on our servers.

Step 7: Completion

Confirmation of successful onboarding. You will receive information about next steps and your pathway forward.

3.2 Onboarding Status

StatusDescription
In ProgressYou are currently completing the onboarding steps
Pending PaymentAll steps complete, awaiting payment processing
CompletedOnboarding fully complete, account activated
ExpiredOnboarding session has expired (requires restart)

5. Progression Pathway

5.1 What You Will See

  • Stage Title & Summary — Clear description of your current stage
  • Focus Area — What you should concentrate on at this stage
  • Actions & Tasks — A checklist of specific tasks to complete
  • Typical Outputs — Examples of the work products expected at your stage
  • Progress to Next Stage — A highlighted box showing requirements to move to the next stage

5.2 Stage Progression

Progressing through stages is managed by TBP administrators. When you have completed the required tasks and demonstrated the necessary capabilities, an administrator may promote you to the next stage. You will receive a notification when your stage changes.

Note: Stage progression is not automatic. It is based on demonstrated capability, task completion, and administrator review.

6. Managing Your Profile

5.1 Accessing Your Profile

  1. Click on your name or avatar in the navigation bar
  2. Select Profile from the dropdown menu
  3. Alternatively, navigate to Dashboard → Profile

5.2 Editing Your Profile

  1. Click the Edit Profile button on your profile page
  2. Update fields: Title, Bio (up to 2000 characters), Years of Experience, Availability, Category, Location, LinkedIn URL, Website URL
  3. Click Save to apply your changes
Tip: A complete profile increases your visibility and helps administrators assign you to suitable projects and work areas.

5.3 Setting a Featured Project

You can set one project as your featured project, which will be prominently displayed on your public profile. Navigate to your profile and use the Featured Project selector.

5.4 Public Strategist Profile

Your public profile page includes:

  • Hero section with your name, title, and stage badge
  • Professional bio
  • Current TBP Projects
  • Strategist Focus card showing your assigned work areas
  • Featured project highlight
  • Recent activity feed

7. Browsing & Managing Projects

6.1 Browse Projects

  1. Navigate to Dashboard → Browse Projects
  2. Browse the list of available TBP projects
  3. Use the search bar to find specific projects
  4. Click on a project to view its details

6.2 Project Details

Each project page includes:

  • Project title and description
  • Category badge (NPNGS Protocols, Infrastructure Systems, etc.)

12. Referral System

The TBP Global Strategist platform includes a comprehensive referral system that rewards you for bringing new members into the network.

12.1 Your Referral Code

Each strategist receives a unique referral code upon account activation. You can find your code in the Referrals section of your dashboard.

12.2 How Referrals Work

  1. Share Your Code — Share your unique referral code with potential new strategists
  2. Registration — When someone registers using your code, they are linked to your account
  3. Payment — Once the referred user completes their fellowship payment, the referral status updates
  4. Hold Period — After a 30-day hold period, referral credits are automatically approved
  5. Wallet Credit — Approved credits appear in your referral wallet
  6. Payout — Request payout of your available balance via bank transfer or PayPal

12.3 Referral Statuses

StatusDescription
Pending RegistrationReferral link used, awaiting user registration
Payment ReceivedReferred user has completed fellowship payment
Waiting Approval30-day hold period in progress
ApprovedReferral credit approved and added to wallet
PaidPayout processed and completed
CancelledReferral cancelled or invalidated

12.4 Referral Wallet

Your referral wallet tracks your earnings:

  • Available Balance — Credits approved and ready for payout
  • Paid Balance — Total amount already paid out to you

12.5 Requesting a Payout

  1. Navigate to Dashboard → Referrals
  2. View your available balance in the wallet section
  3. Click Request Payout
  4. Select your payment method (Bank Transfer or PayPal)
  5. Enter the payout amount
  6. Submit the request
Note: Payout requests are processed by administrators. You will receive a notification when your payout is approved and completed.

12.6 Managing Payment Methods

You can store multiple payment methods for receiving payouts:

  • Bank Transfer — Account holder name, bank name, account number, routing number, SWIFT code, IBAN
  • PayPal — PayPal email address

Navigate to Dashboard → Payment Methods to add, edit, or remove payment methods.

14. Account Settings

14.1 Changing Your Password

  1. Navigate to Dashboard → Settings
  2. Click the Change Password section
  3. Enter your current password, new password, and confirm
  4. Click Save to update
Important: Choose a strong password with a mix of letters, numbers, and special characters.

14.2 Notification Preferences

Manage which types of notifications you receive via Settings. You can enable or disable notifications for stage changes, work area assignments, project updates, collaboration requests, and system announcements.

14.3 Account Information

Your account email and basic information can be viewed in Settings. Contact an administrator to change your email address.

15. Troubleshooting & FAQ

15.1 I cannot log in

Check your email and password. Use Forgot Password to reset. Contact your administrator if the issue persists.

15.2 My dashboard shows the wrong stage

Your stage is managed by TBP administrators. Contact them if you believe your stage is incorrect.

15.3 I cannot see my work areas

Work areas are assigned by administrators. Check the Assigned areas section on your dashboard. Contact your administrator if needed.

15.4 My file upload failed

Ensure your file is in a supported format (PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, CSV, TXT, RTF, ODT, ODS). Check file size limits. Refresh and try again.

15.5 I am not receiving notifications

Check your notification preferences in Settings. Ensure your browser allows notifications. Refresh the page.

15.6 How do I contact support?

Platform support is handled through your TBP organisation administrators. Contact them for account issues, stage progression, or platform questions.

15.7 Can I use Google Sign-In?

Yes. If you have a Google account, you can sign in using the Continue with Google button on the login page.

15.8 How do I message another strategist?

Navigate to Dashboard → Messages and click New Conversation. You can also click the message button on any strategist's profile page.
